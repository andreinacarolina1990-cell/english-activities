💡 Grammar Reference

Past Continuous Action in progress at a past moment.
Structure: was/were + verb-ing
e.g. She was driving.
Past Simple Action that interrupted the ongoing one.
Usually irregular or verb + -ed
e.g. She saw the accident.
was / were I / He / She / It → was
You / We / They → were
Key connector Use "when" to link both actions.
Past Continuous when Past Simple.
Subject + was/were + verb-ing  when  subject + past simple verb.
e.g. They were harvesting when it started to rain.
